01 / ToolsDrawn in Kern

Every curve exactly where you put it.

Autotrace is fine for a start, but type designers want the handles. Place every point yourself, then let Kern do the dull parts: spacing, interpolation, export.

Outlines01
g

Curves with handles

Cubic and quadratic Béziers, smooth and corner points, and a pen that snaps to extremes so curves stay clean at every size.

Kerning02
AV

Pairs, classes, exceptions

Kern by group, then fix the one pair that looks wrong. A live string shows every change in context.

Variable axes03
RrRrRr

One file, every weight

Draw the masters, define weight and width axes, and scrub between them. Kern checks that every master is compatible.

Specimens04
R

Proof sheets that print

Waterfalls, paragraphs and glyph grids, generated from the font as it is right now. Export to PDF for a red pen.

03 / OwnershipNo account needed

    

      
Your fonts are files, not a subscription.

      
Kern saves plain UFO sources on your own disk. Open them in any other editor, put them in git, or hand them to a foundry.
